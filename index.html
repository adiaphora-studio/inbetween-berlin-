Beasts We Hold

Animal forms brought into the staircases of Berlin's bourgeois homes — where ornament, status and imagination became part of everyday life.

Everyday spaces carry more than we see.

In walls, staircases, floors and courtyards, cultures leave traces of what they valued, desired and believed.
